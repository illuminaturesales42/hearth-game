Privacy in Hearth

Last updated 7 August 2026

Hearth is a wellness game about restoring a village. Some of what you put into it is personal, so here is exactly what happens to it — in plain language, with no exceptions hidden further down.

The short version: your game lives on your own device. We do not sell data, we do not run ads, and there is no advertising or tracking network anywhere in Hearth. Nothing you write is read by us for any purpose other than storing and returning it to you.

What stays on your device

Your entire save — your town, your progress, your streaks, your settings, and anything you write in the gratitude journal — is stored in your browser's local storage on the device you play on. If you never enable cloud save, it never leaves that device.

What cloud save sends, if you turn it on

Cloud save exists because mobile browsers delete site storage after about a week of not playing, which would erase your village. When it is on, your save file is uploaded to our server (Cloudflare D1) so it can be restored.

That save file includes the text of your gratitude journal entries. They are stored as ordinary text, not encrypted, in the same record as the rest of your game state. Please keep that in mind before writing anything you would not want stored on a server.

  • The upload is identified by a random key your device generates. There is no account, no email address attached to it, and no name.
  • We do not use the contents of your save for anything except giving it back to your device.
  • Because the key is the only identifier, anyone who obtains your device's key could read that save — treat it like a password.

Health and wellness data

Hearth converts real-world wellness actions into in-game energy. On the web version you record these yourself; the game stores the fact that an action was logged and when. If you later use a version that connects to Apple Health or Google Fit, that data is read on your device to award energy and is not uploaded to us.

Weather

The sky in your town reflects real weather. To do this the game may request a forecast from Open-Meteo, optionally using your approximate location if you grant permission. Location is used for that request only and is not stored on our servers.

Analytics

We may collect anonymous gameplay events (for example: a day was completed, a chapter was reached) to understand whether the game is working. These events contain no journal text, no health details, and no advertising identifiers.

What we never do

  • No advertising, and no ad or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • No selling or sharing of your data with third parties.
  • No selling of in-game energy — the game's core pillar.

Your control

  • Export: Settings → Export gives you your entire save as a file you keep.
  • Delete: clearing site data in your browser erases the local copy completely.
  • Delete from the server: email us at the address below and we will remove the stored save for your device key.

Children

Hearth is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect data from them.
